Profile: your changes were not saved

What it means

When you edit your profile, each value must be a believable number: age 13–100 years, height 100–250 cm, weight 20–300 kg. Sex must be Male or Female. One of them was missing or outside that range, so nothing was saved.

What happens if you ignore it

Your profile stays as it was before you tapped Edit profile. Tap Cancel to leave it that way.

How to fix it

  1. Correct the value the message names. Type only the number; a comma works too.
  2. Tap Save again.

Body fat: method not recorded

What it means

This body-fat reading was saved as a percentage only, without saying how it was measured and without neck and waist numbers. The app does not guess: it is not shown as a tape reading, it gets no margin of error, and it is drawn as its own series in the Body fat chart.

What happens if you ignore it

Nothing is lost. The reading stays in your history and still counts as your latest body fat. It just cannot be compared like for like with your other readings, and it has no margin of error, because both depend on the method.

How to fix it

  1. In Profile, scroll to History and find the reading that says Method not recorded.
  2. Open Set the method… on that row and choose DEXA scan or AI photo estimate.

The row changes where it is and joins that method's line in the chart. A tape or caliper reading needs its measurements: delete the row and log it again with them.

Body fat: outside this method's range

What it means

Tape (Navy) and caliper readings are most reliable between 15 % and 30 % body fat. This reading is below 15 % or above 30 %, where both methods get less precise. Nothing is wrong with what you entered, and nothing is missing.

What happens if you ignore it

The reading is kept and plotted as usual. Its margin of error (the ± figure on the row) is wider than in the middle of the range, so a small change between two such readings may not be a real change.

How to fix it

  1. There is nothing to correct. Read the trend over several readings instead of one.
  2. For a tighter figure at this body-fat level, log a DEXA scan in Profile under the measurement method.
